Privacy

Privacy Notice

Information we collect

When you request a quotation, Aircon Partner may collect your name, business name, telephone number, email address, business postcode and information about your air-conditioning project.

Why we use your information

We use the information you provide to assess your quotation request, identify a suitable air-conditioning installation company and facilitate contact about the project you have asked us to help with.

Sharing your information

Where appropriate, we may share the details of your quotation request with a suitable air-conditioning installation company covering your location and project type. That company may then contact you directly about your project.

Marketing

Submitting a quotation request does not automatically subscribe you to unrelated marketing communications.

Retention and your rights

We keep quotation information only for as long as reasonably necessary for lead administration, quality control, dispute handling, record keeping and applicable legal obligations. Depending on the circumstances, you may have rights in relation to your personal information, including access, correction, deletion, restriction and objection.

Contact

For privacy questions, email hello@airconpartner.co.uk.
